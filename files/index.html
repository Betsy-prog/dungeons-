What is D&D?

Dungeons & Dragons is a cooperative storytelling game. You create a character, team up with friends, and go on adventures together — battling monsters, solving puzzles, and making choices that shape the story.

🎲

Roll Dice

A 20-sided die (d20) decides if your actions succeed or fail. High roll = good. Low roll = trouble!

📖

Tell a Story

One player is the Dungeon Master. They describe the world. Everyone else decides what to do next.

⚔️

Fight & Survive

When a fight breaks out, players take turns attacking. Teamwork is the key to winning.

How to Play

D&D has a simple rhythm. Once you get it, everything clicks.

01

Create Your Character

Pick a Race (Human, Elf, Dwarf…) and a Class (Fighter, Wizard…). Roll dice to set your stats. Give your character a name and backstory.

02

The DM Sets the Scene

The Dungeon Master describes the world around you. Your group decides what to do — explore, fight, or negotiate. There are no wrong answers.

03

Roll for Everything

Trying to pick a lock? Climb a wall? Persuade a guard? Roll a d20 and add your stat bonus. Beat the Difficulty Class (DC) and you succeed!

04

Fight in Rounds

Combat is turn-based. Everyone rolls Initiative to see who goes first. On your turn: Move, then take an Action (attack, cast a spell, help a teammate).

05

Rest & Level Up

After a battle, rest to recover HP. Earn enough XP and your character levels up — getting stronger stats and powerful new abilities.
